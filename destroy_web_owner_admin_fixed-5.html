Destroy any page

Type any website address and its text becomes a level you can shoot to pieces. Leave the address empty to use the text box: the first line is the title.

Run with A and D. Space jumps, hold it to fly. S drops through. Click to shoot, right-click or E for a grenade. Keys 1 to 9 and 0, the weapon grid or the mouse wheel switch between 12 weapons. Shift dashes. The game has 10 normal abilities: Q sun, F shield, C rune circle, V meteors, Z heal, X freeze, B black hole, T storm, G drone, R nuke. Extra admin-only powers are available from the Owner Panel. After wave 5 your max health doubles to 200. Wave 5 takes you to the Moon (no web page, low gravity). After wave 10 you warp to the Galaxy. Wave 13 unlocks Auto-Aim, and wave 25 unlocks Energy Blast. Sometimes a golden time crystal appears: touch it to reset every power timer. Bots arrive in waves, and a destroyed page is rebuilt for the next wave. On touch, tap the page to shoot and use the buttons.
